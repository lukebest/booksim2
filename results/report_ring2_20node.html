5. How to read the comparison

  • S0 is the reactive baseline: in-ring never stalls, I-tag bounds inject starvation, E-tag (rebound onto reserved eject entries — an adaptation, not HiRD) bounds leave livelock.
  • S1 feeds board/leave failure counts back to the source and AIMDs the token-bucket rate. On a closed burst this often hurts makespan because every source sees board NACK in the first epoch and the rate collapses; that is a result, not a bug.
  • S2 deletes station storage (zero distributed bits) and pays an arbiter plus a small control-plane tax. Whether it lands on the Pareto front depends on t_sched_cycles — an algorithm that is only fast because it is unbuildable is charged for that.
